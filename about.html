Company profile公司简介

Technology · Manufacturing · Quality科技缔造 · 专业品质

Tianchang Xianrui New Energy Technology Co., Ltd. (brand: Senrex) is a technology-based enterprise deeply involved in the lithium battery industry chain. The company is located in the New Energy Industry Base of Tianchang City, Anhui Province.

天长市先锐新能源科技有限公司（品牌：赛恩力 Senrex）是一家深耕锂电池产业链的科技型企业，公司坐落于安徽省天长市新能源产业基地。

Lithium batteries are our core business. We produce and sell photovoltaic modules, inverters and related products, and we also design and build solar power and electrical engineering projects. With a complete R&D and testing system, we master the core technologies behind high energy density and long cycle life, and our products meet strict performance requirements.

公司以锂电池产业为核心方向，主营光伏组件、逆变器等产品生产销售，同时承接光伏发电及电力工程设计施工业务。依托完善的研发测试体系，公司掌握了支撑高能量密度、长循环寿命电池需求的核心技术，产品性能满足严苛要求。

As an important participant in Tianchang's 10-billion-yuan new energy vehicle parts industrial base, Xianrui New Energy is part of the local new energy industrial cluster. We stay technology-driven and quality-first, and aim to become an influential company in the regional new energy field.

作为天长市百亿新能源汽车零部件产业基地的重要参与者，先锐新能源积极融入本地新能源产业集群，借助完善的协作配套与产业政策优势，持续提升自主创新能力。未来，公司将继续坚持科技驱动、质量为本，致力于成为区域新能源领域具有较强影响力的企业。
